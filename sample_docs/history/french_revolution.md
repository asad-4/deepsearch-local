# The French Revolution (1789-1799)

Causes: royal debt, food shortages, an unfair tax system and Enlightenment
ideas about rights and equality.

Key events:
- **1789:** The Estates-General meets; the Third Estate forms the National
  Assembly. On 14 July, crowds storm the Bastille.
- **1789:** Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen.
- **1793:** King Louis XVI is executed.
- **1793-1794:** The Reign of Terror under Robespierre and the Committee of
  Public Safety; thousands are executed by guillotine.
- **1799:** Napoleon Bonaparte takes power in a coup, ending the revolution.

The revolution spread the ideas of liberty, equality and nationalism across
Europe.
