# Study Tips

- **Spaced repetition:** review material at increasing intervals (1 day,
  3 days, a week). Flashcard apps schedule this for you.
- **Active recall:** close your notes and try to write down what you remember
  before checking. Testing yourself beats rereading.
- **Pomodoro technique:** 25 minutes of focused work, then a 5 minute break.
- **Interleaving:** mix different topics in one session instead of studying one
  topic for hours.
- Before an exam, do past papers under timed conditions and sleep well the
  night before.
