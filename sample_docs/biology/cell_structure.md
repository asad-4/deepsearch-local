# Cell Structure

All cells have a cell membrane, cytoplasm and DNA. Eukaryotic cells also have
membrane-bound organelles:

- **Nucleus:** holds the DNA and controls the cell.
- **Mitochondria:** the "powerhouse of the cell"; they produce ATP through
  cellular respiration.
- **Ribosomes:** build proteins.
- **Endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi apparatus:** process and ship proteins.
- **Chloroplasts (plants only):** carry out photosynthesis.
- **Cell wall (plants only):** made of cellulose, gives support.

Prokaryotic cells, such as bacteria, have no nucleus.
