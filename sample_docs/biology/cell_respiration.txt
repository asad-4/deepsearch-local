Cellular Respiration

Cellular respiration releases the energy stored in glucose and stores it as ATP,
the energy currency of the cell. Most of it happens in the mitochondria.

Stages:
1. Glycolysis - in the cytoplasm. Glucose is split into two pyruvate molecules,
   producing 2 ATP.
2. Krebs cycle (citric acid cycle) - in the mitochondrial matrix. Produces NADH
   and FADH2 and releases carbon dioxide.
3. Electron transport chain - on the inner mitochondrial membrane. Uses NADH and
   FADH2 to pump protons; ATP synthase then makes about 34 ATP. Oxygen is the
   final electron acceptor and forms water.

Without oxygen, cells use fermentation, which produces far less ATP.
