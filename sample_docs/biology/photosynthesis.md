# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is how plants, algae and some bacteria turn light energy into
chemical energy. It happens in the chloroplast.

6 CO2 + 6 H2O + light -> C6H12O6 (glucose) + 6 O2

## Light-dependent reactions
Take place in the thylakoid membranes. Chlorophyll absorbs light, mostly red
and blue wavelengths, which is why leaves look green. Water is split, releasing
oxygen, and the energy is stored in ATP and NADPH.

## Calvin cycle (light-independent reactions)
Takes place in the stroma. The enzyme RuBisCO fixes carbon dioxide, and ATP and
NADPH from the light reactions are used to build glucose.
